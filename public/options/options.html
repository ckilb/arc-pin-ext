Loose bookmarks become pinned tabs. Each folder inside becomes a tab group, and deeper folders join the group they sit in.

    Behaviour

    Click the toolbar icon on a pinned tab to return it to its bookmark (or reload it there); on any other tab it adds the tab as a pin. Right-click the icon for Sync now and Make this the pinned window.

    Pins live in one window. Other windows are left alone.
